# A轮融资主要条款清单 / Series A Principal Terms

**PROJECT AURORA — SIMULATED LEGAL WORK PRODUCT**  
*Fictional training sample; not client advice.*

## 交易性质 / Nature

本清单为投资人律师模拟审阅稿，未获双方签署；除明确标明的约束性条款外，均属待谈商业条款。

**English**

Investor-counsel drafting exercise only; unsigned. Commercial terms remain subject to negotiation except any provisions expressly designated binding.

## 认购及估值 / Subscription and valuation

投资人拟以人民币3,000万元认购新增股权；投前2.2亿元，投后2.5亿元；在无额外稀释前提下全部交割后占12%。

**English**

RMB30 million subscription for newly issued equity; RMB220 million pre-money and RMB250 million post-money; 12% fully funded post-closing, absent additional dilution.

## 交割 / Closings

第一期2,000万元；第二期1,000万元。每期的增资、股东名册、章程及登记事项与实际缴款相匹配。第二期不自动视为已支付。

**English**

RMB20 million first closing and RMB10 million second closing. Each tranche is reflected in relevant corporate documents upon actual funding; no deemed payment of Tranche 2.

## 第二期先决条件 / Second-closing CP

（i）重要陈述保证于交割日持续真实；（ii）无符合合同定义的重大不利变化；（iii）更新尽调完成；（iv）可核验的有效增信安排完成；（v）无重大违约；（vi）投资决策机构最终批准。仅投资人有权书面豁免其利益条件。

**English**

Material bring-down of warranties; no contractually defined MAC; satisfactory targeted update DD; documented and verifiable credit support; no material breach; final IC approval. Waiver of investor-benefit CP requires express written notice.

## 回购 / Founder purchase

若2030年12月31日前未完成合格IPO，投资人可要求创始人按协议受让其全部或部分股权。目标公司不作为本拟议条款的回购义务人。

**English**

If no Qualified IPO by 31 December 2030, the investor may require the founder to purchase all or part of the investor equity. The issuer is not a repurchase obligor in this proposed draft.

## 价格 / Purchase price

按每笔实际支付投资款分别计息：该笔本金×(1+8%)^(实际付款日至回购价款支付日天数/365)，扣除对应已取得现金分红。避免按全部3,000万元统一起算。

**English**

Calculate separately for each funded tranche: paid principal × 1.08^(actual days from funding to repurchase payment / 365), less attributable cash dividends. No single accrual start date for all RMB30 million.

## 增信 / Credit support

拟由创始人姐姐提供个人保证，但其财产及负债资料尚需核验；原拟追加的商业房产抵押未获B银行同意，不能视为已设立担保。

**English**

Proposed personal guarantee by founder’s sister remains subject to financial and legal verification. Bank B has not consented to a further mortgage; no such mortgage is treated as perfected.

## 信息与上市 / Information and IPO

按季度提供经营财务资料；重大新增负债、担保、查封和执行事项及时通知。IPO申报前依届时规则清理特殊权利，不预设终止后自动恢复。

**English**

Quarterly financial reporting and prompt material debt, guarantee and enforcement notices. Special rights to be reviewed before IPO filing under then-applicable rules; no automatic revival is assumed.

## 文件效力 / Binding status

保密、排他（如最终约定）、费用、适用法和争议解决可单独约定具有约束力；本模拟稿并未假设排他协议已成立。

**English**

Confidentiality, exclusivity (if agreed), expenses, governing law and dispute resolution may be made binding separately; no existing exclusivity arrangement is assumed.
