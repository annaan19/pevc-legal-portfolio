# Investment Agreement — Annotated Redline

> Fictional investor-side legal work sample; not legal advice.

## Reading convention

[DELETE] indicates original language proposed for deletion. [ADD] indicates investor counsel proposed language. This is a clause-level educational redline, not a full executed investment agreement.

## Clause 4 — funding

[DELETE] Investor shall pay all RMB30m upon signing. [ADD] The RMB30m subscription is payable in two tranches: RMB20m at First Closing and RMB10m at Second Closing. The relevant capital increase, shareholder register and registration documents shall reflect equity actually subscribed and funded at each closing, with a fully diluted 12% aggregate investor interest only following both closings and absent intervening dilution.

## Clause 4.3 — second closing CP

[ADD] Payment of the second tranche is conditional upon: (i) material accuracy of representations and warranties; (ii) no material adverse change to the core business, assets or going concern; (iii) disclosure of founder material indebtedness, guarantees and enforcement; (iv) legally documented and investor-accepted effective credit enhancement; (v) satisfactory targeted update DD without newly discovered material undisclosed risk; (vi) no material breach; and (vii) required final internal investment approval. Investor may defer, request cure or expressly waive a condition in writing. Termination of the unpaid tranche requires the defined material contractual trigger and written notice; it is not automatic for every new risk.

## Clause 8.1 — repurchase obligor

[DELETE] Issuer and founder shall jointly repurchase all investor equity. [ADD] If no Qualified IPO by 31 December 2030, investor may require the founder to purchase all or part of the investor equity in accordance with the definitive agreement. Issuer is not a repurchase obligor under this proposed structure. Founder liability and any third-party guarantee shall be separately and expressly documented.

## Clause 8.2 — price

[DELETE] Subscription principal plus 8% annual return. [ADD] For each funded tranche i, Repurchase Price_i = Paid Principal_i × (1.08)^(D_i/365) − Cash Dividends_i, where D_i is the actual number of days between payment of tranche i and the actual repurchase payment date; cash dividends must be attributable to the equity repurchased and shall not be deducted twice. Partial repurchase to be allocated pro rata or as expressly agreed. Payment due 30 days after valid notice, with default interest and transfer mechanics to be separately negotiated.

## Clause 8.3 — disclosure

[DELETE] Founder shall promptly disclose material liabilities. [ADD] Founder shall notify investor in writing within three business days of becoming aware of: (i) new single debt/guarantee ≥RMB3m; (ii) rolling 12-month aggregate ≥RMB8m; (iii) major asset disposals or encumbrances; (iv) seizure, freeze or enforcement; (v) material disputes, default or acceleration; and (vi) other events materially impairing payment ability. Related transactions shall be aggregated. Founder shall not unreasonably dissipate major assets to materially impair repurchase performance.

## Clause 8.4 — guarantee and separate undertaking

[DELETE] The guarantee shall remain effective only if the guarantor continuously supplies asset information. [ADD] The guarantor shall enter into a separate written guarantee and information undertaking specifying guarantee form, scope, term, payment obligations and notice details. The guarantor shall provide material updates and reasonable supporting documents. Failure to provide information constitutes a covenant breach with contractual cure and other remedies; it does not by itself automatically terminate an otherwise valid guarantee. Whether the guarantee is adequate for Second Closing is a distinct investor acceptance question.

## Clause 8.5 — IPO special rights

[DELETE] Repurchase right is suspended at IPO filing and automatically revived if IPO fails. [ADD] Prior to IPO filing, parties shall review and, where required under then-effective law or regulatory requirements, terminate or amend special shareholder rights and execute necessary instruments. Rights actually terminated for compliance shall not automatically revive solely because IPO is withdrawn, rejected or unsuccessful. Temporary non-exercise shall not substitute for a required legal clearing.

## Clause 8.6 — proportional remedies

[ADD] A non-material first-time information default may be cured within three business days after written notice. A materially adverse underlying event may trigger a request for additional reasonable enhancement. Intentional concealment, material misstatement or refusal to cure coupled with material impairment may constitute material breach. Remedies remain subject to the contract and applicable law; do not automatically accelerate repurchase for every minor notice failure.

## Drafting notes

Confirm guarantee parties actually sign the relevant instrument; define Qualified IPO, Business Day, Material Adverse Change, and materiality thresholds; specify corporate approval/registration timing, partial repurchase mechanics, and waiver authority; reconcile all references with the term sheet and cap table.
