**English**

RMB8 million dispute, estimated loss RMB3–5 million and 8% revenue decline disclosed. [MONITOR] No automatic MAC conclusion.

**English**

New borrowings RMB4.5 million and RMB1.8 million total RMB6.3 million, below the proposed RMB8 million aggregate threshold; the RMB4.5 million item separately exceeds the RMB3 million single-item threshold.

**English**

[BLOCKING] Sister guarantee documentation does not substitute for incomplete guarantor DD; Bank B rejected additional mortgage.

【阻断】投资人可接受的有效增信尚未被证明满足合同客观条件；不得仅以条款中出现“有效增信”四字视为满足。

**English**

**English**
