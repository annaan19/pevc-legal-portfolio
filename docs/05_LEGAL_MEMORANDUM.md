# 法律问题备忘录 / Legal Issues Memorandum

**PROJECT AURORA — SIMULATED LEGAL WORK PRODUCT**  
*Fictional training sample; not client advice.*

## 致 / To

远山成长基金投资委员会（虚构）｜Yuanshan Growth Fund Investment Committee (fictional)

## 问题 / Issue

拟议3,000万元A轮融资中，创始人回购、资产负担及第三方保证能否提供充分的投资人退出保障？

**English**

Whether founder purchase obligations, encumbered assets and third-party guarantee provide adequate investor protection for a proposed RMB30 million Series A investment.

## 简要结论 / Short answer

【有条件推进】回购条款不应被当作确定可收回的现金保障。建议采用创始人受让、分期出资、可核验增信及持续披露的组合；在第二期先决条件未满足前不放款。

**English**

[PROCEED SUBJECT TO CONDITIONS] Founder purchase is not assured cash recovery. Use staged funding, verified credit support and continuing covenants. Withhold Tranche 2 pending CP satisfaction.

## 法律分析一｜效力 / Validity

须分别分析合同成立与效力、义务主体的法定限制及股权转让手续。不能简单断言“所有对赌无效”或“约定有效即必然获得回购款”。

**English**

Distinguish contract validity, statutory limits on performance and equity transfer mechanics. Neither all VAMs are automatically invalid nor a valid covenant guarantees collection.

## 法律分析二｜执行 / Enforcement

创始人既有质押、抵押及个人债务削弱回购履约能力。按全部投资款在五年前支付的粗略情景，3,000万元×1.08^5≈4,408万元；本案两期付款须分别计算，不能把该情景金额写成实际到期债务。

**English**

Existing encumbrances weaken recourse. RMB30m×1.08^5≈RMB44.08m is illustrative only if all funds were paid five years earlier. Actual liability must accrue separately for each funded tranche.

## 法律分析三｜增信 / Credit enhancement

姐姐初步可识别个人净资产约2,250万元，不能据此认定覆盖全部回购价款；其公司账面净资产及应收账款并非可直接执行的个人现金资产。B银行未同意追加抵押。

**English**

Sister’s preliminary identifiable personal net assets of RMB22.5 million do not establish full coverage. Corporate book equity and receivables are not personal liquid assets. Bank B has not consented to further mortgage.

## 法律分析四｜上市 / IPO

对投资人特殊权利应在IPO申报前依据届时适用规则审查、必要时真实清理；不得仅凭“自动中止、失败后恢复”推定合规。

**English**

Review investor special rights before IPO filing under applicable rules; do not assume automatic suspension-and-revival is sufficient.

## 建议 / Next steps

补齐股本及出资文件；完成保证人资产负债核查；修订交割条件和价格公式；落实书面投资决策；对未完成事项保留明确的OPEN标记。

**English**

Complete capital DD, guarantor financial checks, CP and price drafting, written IC approval and an open-issues log.

**English**

For live use, verify current PRC Company Law, Civil Code security rules, judicial practice and IPO regulations. This document is not live legal advice.
