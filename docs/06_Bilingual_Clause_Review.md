# Bilingual Clause Review — Original Drafting

> Fictional investor-side legal work sample; not legal advice.

## 1. Representations and Warranties / 陈述与保证

EN: Each of the Company and the Founder represents and warrants, severally as to matters concerning itself or himself/herself, that the information disclosed in the Disclosure Schedule is true, accurate and complete in all material respects as of the date hereof and, subject to the agreed bring-down standard, as of the relevant Closing Date. CN: 目标公司及创始股东分别就与自身有关的事项陈述并保证：披露清单所载信息于本协议签署日及按照约定持续确认标准确定的相关交割日在所有重大方面真实、准确、完整。 REVIEW: Identify whose warranty, knowledge qualifiers, disclosure schedule exceptions and whether materiality is already embedded to avoid double materiality.

## 2. Conditions Precedent / 交割先决条件

EN: The Investor’s obligation to fund the Second Tranche is subject to the satisfaction or express written waiver by the Investor of each Second Closing Condition, including completion of an effective credit enhancement arrangement acceptable to the Investor under the agreed objective documentation requirements. CN: 投资方支付第二期投资款的义务，以各项第二期交割条件均已满足或经投资方明确书面豁免为前提，其中包括按照约定的客观文件要求完成投资方认可的有效增信安排。 REVIEW: Separate satisfaction, evidence and waiver; avoid an unqualified subjective “sole satisfaction” standard; state consequences if CP remains unmet.

## 3. Repurchase and Notice / 回购及通知

EN: If a Qualified IPO has not occurred by 31 December 2030, the Investor may require the Founder to purchase the Investor’s Shares in accordance with Clause 8. The Founder shall notify the Investor within three Business Days after becoming aware of any event specified in Clause 8.3. CN: 如目标公司截至2030年12月31日未完成合格首次公开发行并上市，投资方有权依第8条要求创始股东受让投资方所持股权。创始股东应于知悉第8.3条所列事项后三个工作日内通知投资方。 REVIEW: “Repurchase” is a transaction label, but actual mechanism is founder purchase of equity; specify transfer approvals, partial purchase, price and due date.

## 4. Confidentiality / 保密

EN: Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information received in connection with the Transaction, except information that becomes public without breach or is required to be disclosed by law or a competent authority, provided that prior notice is given where legally permitted and reasonably practicable. CN: 各方应对因本次交易取得的非公开信息承担保密义务，但非因违约而公开的信息或依法、依有权机关要求必须披露的信息除外；在法律允许且合理可行的情况下，披露方应事先通知相关方。 REVIEW: Distinguish carve-outs, permitted recipients, required disclosures and advance notice.

## English Executive Summary

Project Aurora is a fictional RMB30 million Series A investment in a PRC AI software company. The investor’s principal legal concerns are the founder’s limited demonstrated ability to satisfy a potential equity repurchase obligation, existing encumbrances over key assets, uncertainty regarding third-party guarantee support, and the treatment of special shareholder rights in a future IPO. We recommend a two-tranche investment structure, founder-only repurchase as ancillary protection, verifiable second-closing conditions, ongoing disclosure covenants and an IPO pre-filing special-rights review. Based on the simulated update due diligence, the investor may proceed in principle but should withhold the RMB10 million second tranche until acceptable credit enhancement and final internal approvals are documented.
