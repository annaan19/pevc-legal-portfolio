# 法律尽调重大问题清单 / Red Flag Due Diligence

**PROJECT AURORA — SIMULATED LEGAL WORK PRODUCT**  
*Fictional training sample; not client advice.*

## RF-01｜创始人偿债能力 / Founder recourse

【高风险】创始人投后持股58%，其中相当于目标公司总股本35%的股权已向A银行质押，对应约4,000万元个人债务。质押股权不能直接按未设负担股权估值。

**English**

[HIGH] Founder holds 58% post-closing; equity representing 35% of the issuer’s total equity is pledged to Bank A against approximately RMB40 million personal debt. Encumbered shares are not freely available recovery assets.

## RF-02｜房产 / Property

【高风险】商业房产初步价值2,300万元，B银行贷款余额1,200万元；账面差额约1,100万元不等于可实现抵押价值。B银行拒绝追加抵押。

**English**

[HIGH] Preliminary property value RMB23 million less RMB12 million bank debt suggests only RMB11 million before costs and enforcement discounts. Bank B rejected the additional mortgage.

## RF-03｜第三方保证 / Third-party guarantee

【待核查】姐姐名下存款及理财600万元、无抵押住宅1,800万元、其他资产150万元、负债300万元，初步个人净资产2,250万元（未计其公司股权）。其独资公司启明科技账面净资产3,000万元，其中应收账款2,000万元；银行借款1,200万元原则上已反映在净资产，不得再次简单扣减。

**English**

[OPEN] Sister’s preliminary personal net assets: RMB22.5 million excluding her company equity. Qiming Technology reports RMB30 million book net assets, including RMB20 million receivables; its RMB12 million bank debt should not be deducted twice without accounting reconciliation.

## RF-04｜目标公司争议 / Company litigation

【持续跟踪】目标公司涉诉请求金额800万元，律师初步估损300万—500万元；未对核心资产采取保全。另有近三个月收入下降8%，但主要客户及现金流相对稳定。现有事实不足以自动认定MAC。

**English**

[MONITOR] Claim amount RMB8 million, estimated exposure RMB3–5 million, no attachment of core assets. Revenue declined 8% over three months but key customers and cash flow are reportedly stable; no automatic MAC conclusion.

## RF-05｜出资与权属 / Capital and title

【未完成】需取得工商档案、历次增资文件、出资证明、股权质押登记、公司章程及优先认购权放弃文件。

**English**

[OPEN] Obtain corporate filings, contribution evidence, pledge searches, articles and pre-emption waivers.

## 处置意见 / Recommendation

【暂缓第二期】在增信文件可执行性、保证人尽调和投资决策最终批准均完成前，不建议支付第二期1,000万元。

**English**

[HOLD TRANCHE 2] Do not release RMB10 million until credit support, guarantor verification and final IC approval are documented.
