# Red Flag Due Diligence Report

> Fictional investor-side legal work sample; not legal advice.

## Executive view

PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS. Founder repurchase is an ancillary exit protection, not a substitute for financial underwriting. Second tranche should not fund until investor-acceptable enhancement and approvals are demonstrably complete.

## RF-01 Founder repayment capacity — HIGH

Founder holds 58% of issuer post-close, while equity representing 35% of total issuer equity is already pledged to Bank A against approximately RMB40m personal operating debt. Founder property preliminary value RMB23m, Bank B outstanding RMB12m; the remaining equity and property cannot be counted at nominal value as readily available collateral. Action: title/pledge registration searches, bank confirmations, debt schedules, valuation and enforcement/litigation checks; reassess recourse and avoid relying on repurchase alone.

## RF-02 Property encumbrance — HIGH

RMB23m preliminary property valuation less RMB12m disclosed bank debt suggests only a rough RMB11m cushion before interest, costs and discount; this is not realizable collateral value. Bank B refuses proposed additional mortgage. Action: do not describe mortgage as established; replace with documented, independently assessed enhancement or investor waiver.

## RF-03 Sister guarantee — MEDIUM/HIGH

Sister: RMB6m deposits/wealth assets, RMB18m unencumbered residence, RMB1.5m other assets, RMB3m liabilities = RMB22.5m preliminary identifiable net assets, excluding her 100% equity in Qiming. Qiming book net assets RMB30m, including RMB20m receivables; RMB12m bank borrowings. Because net assets already deduct liabilities in principle, do not deduct bank debt again without reconciling accounts. Action: verify title, liquidity, debt completeness, receivables aging, cash collections, bank covenants, equity valuation and guarantee execution; conditional acceptance only as part of package.

## RF-04 Second closing legal dispute — MONITOR

Issuer commercial dispute claim RMB8m, counsel estimated loss RMB3–5m, no preservation of core assets. Revenue down 8% over three months, key customers and cash flow stable. Facts alone do not automatically establish a MAC or contractual breach. Action: litigation update, legal counsel letter, liquidity assessment and specific CP analysis.

## RF-05 Capital contribution and title — OPEN DD

No underlying registration files, contribution receipts, shareholder approvals or complete encumbrance register supplied in fictional scenario. Action: obtain cap table backup, articles, resolutions, contribution certificates, equity pledge/freeze searches and pre-emption waivers; keep open rather than asserting compliance.

## Closing status

Blocking: effective enhancement CP unfulfilled; sister DD incomplete; bank refuses proposed mortgage; investment committee approval remains conditional. Monitor: disclosed founder debts RMB4.5m and RMB1.8m (cumulative RMB6.3m, below hypothetical RMB8m rolling threshold) and commercial litigation. No second tranche payment yet.
