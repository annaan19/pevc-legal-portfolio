# 中英文合同条款审阅 / Bilingual Clause Review

**PROJECT AURORA — SIMULATED LEGAL WORK PRODUCT**  
*Fictional training sample; not client advice.*

## 陈述与保证 / Representations and warranties

目标公司及创始人分别就与自身有关的事项陈述并保证：除披露函列明的例外外，相关信息在签署日及适用交割日在所有重大方面真实、准确、完整。
【审阅要点】明确各自责任、披露例外及重大性标准，避免重复重大性限定。

**English**

Each of the Company and the Founder represents and warrants, severally in respect of matters concerning itself or himself/herself, that, except as disclosed in the Disclosure Schedule, the relevant information is true, accurate and complete in all material respects as of signing and the applicable Closing Date.
[REVIEW] Identify obligor, disclosed exceptions and materiality; avoid double materiality.

## 第二期交割条件 / Second-closing condition

投资人支付第二期投资款的义务，以约定交割条件均满足或由投资人明确书面豁免为前提，包括完成符合约定文件标准的有效增信安排。
【审阅要点】“完成增信”应列明证明文件、签署主体及可执行性标准。

**English**

The Investor’s obligation to fund the Second Tranche is subject to satisfaction or the Investor’s express written waiver of each Second Closing Condition, including completion of legally documented credit support meeting the agreed evidentiary requirements.
[REVIEW] Specify documentary evidence, signing parties and enforceability.

## 创始人受让 / Founder purchase

如截至2030年12月31日未完成合格IPO，投资人可要求创始人按第8条约定受让投资人全部或部分股权。
【审阅要点】界定合格IPO、通知、付款期限、股权转让手续及部分受让计算。

**English**

If a Qualified IPO has not occurred by 31 December 2030, the Investor may require the Founder to purchase all or part of the Investor Equity pursuant to Clause 8.
[REVIEW] Define Qualified IPO, notice, payment deadline, transfer mechanics and partial purchase calculation.

## 保密 / Confidentiality

各方对本交易中取得的非公开信息承担保密义务；依法或依有权机关要求披露的，在法律允许且合理可行时应提前通知。
【审阅要点】补充获准披露对象、保密期限及法定例外。

**English**

Each Party shall keep non-public information obtained in connection with the Transaction confidential. Where disclosure is required by law or competent authority, prior notice shall be given to the extent legally permitted and reasonably practicable.
[REVIEW] Add permitted recipients, duration and statutory exceptions.

## 术语核对 / Terminology

交割先决条件—Conditions Precedent；重大不利变化—Material Adverse Change；陈述与保证—Representations and Warranties；披露函—Disclosure Schedule/Disclosure Letter（应统一）；创始人受让—Founder Purchase（避免误指公司回购）。

**English**

Conditions Precedent; Material Adverse Change; Representations and Warranties; Disclosure Schedule/Disclosure Letter (use consistently); Founder Purchase (avoid suggesting issuer repurchase).
