# Closing Checklist & Investment Committee Summary

> Fictional investor-side legal work sample; not legal advice.

## Decision

PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS. Do not release RMB10m Second Tranche today. The original RMB20m First Tranche is assumed funded solely for this simulated exercise.

## Satisfied / monitored

Update DD delivered; disclosed RMB8m dispute with estimated RMB3–5m loss and no core asset preservation; 8% revenue decline but stable cash flow/customers; founder RMB4.5m and RMB1.8m new borrowing disclosed (RMB6.3m total); existing Bank A and B debt performing; supplemental transaction documents signed. No automatic MAC conclusion from these facts.

## Blocking item 1

Sister personal guarantee signed but supporting personal assets/liabilities DD and Qiming receivable review incomplete. Action: obtain asset title, liquid asset statements, liabilities, guarantees, litigation checks, Qiming receivables aging, collections and debt schedules; assess practical enforceability and acceptability.

## Alternative required

Bank B refuses additional mortgage on founder commercial property. Action: remove assumption that property security is perfected; assess alternative credit support, potentially separately documented first-ranking collateral over an eligible unencumbered asset if legally and commercially feasible, or investor waiver.

## Blocking item 2

Second-closing CP expressly requires effective investor-acceptable enhancement; it is not satisfied merely because the contract contains the words “effective enhancement”. Action: written legal/credit sign-off on completed security package or authorized waiver.

## Pending approval

Investment committee principle approval is subject to implementation of enhancement. Action: confirm final approval authority, evidence and release instructions after CP satisfaction.

## Negotiation points

Investor asks for enforceable downside protection and CP. Founder seeks to limit personal recourse and avoid over-restrictive covenants. Possible compromise: objective information triggers, reasonable cure periods, staged funding, a properly assessed third-party guarantee and commercial valuation adjustment only if agreed. Avoid presenting unagreed valuation changes as executed terms.

## Decision matrix

Option A: original one-time RMB30m + issuer/founder joint repurchase — NOT RECOMMENDED. Option B: founder-only repurchase without verified enhancement — NOT RECOMMENDED. Option C: staged RMB20m/RMB10m + founder-only repurchase + verified enhancement + IPO rights review — RECOMMENDED SUBJECT TO CP. Each option remains subject to commercial negotiation and current legal verification.

## Interview-ready explanation

The central issue was not whether an 8% repurchase formula could be drafted; it was whether the investor could actually collect, whether existing pledges and mortgages undermined collateral, and whether issuer-level obligations could complicate an IPO. We therefore shifted from a single contractual remedy to a verifiable package of staged funding, credit support and ongoing disclosure.
