# Series A Term Sheet — Investor-Side Draft

> Fictional investor-side legal work sample; not legal advice.

## Parties and financing

Issuer: 星澜智能科技有限公司 (fictional PRC LLC). Investor: 远山成长基金 (fictional). Subscription: RMB30m in newly issued equity, pre-money valuation RMB220m, post-money RMB250m, investor fully diluted post-closing interest 12%, assuming no other dilution.

## Two closings

First closing: RMB20m, resulting in 8.3333% immediately after first closing. Second closing: RMB10m, resulting in 12% total after second closing, subject to agreed CP. Capitalization and registration mechanics to be documented consistently; no deemed funding or equity issuance for unpaid second tranche.

## Conditions precedent

First closing: authority, valid resolutions and waiver of applicable pre-emption rights; satisfactory legal DD; signed definitive documents; accurate core warranties; completion of agreed corporate filings as applicable. Second closing: updated warranties, no material adverse change, targeted update DD, no undisclosed material founder liabilities, effective investor-acceptable credit enhancement, no material breach and final investor approval. Waiver only by written investor notice.

## Governance and information rights

Quarterly management accounts and material-event notifications, subject to confidentiality and proportionality. One investor board observer (non-voting), subject to later IPO special-rights review. Reserved matters and voting thresholds to be negotiated; not assumed agreed.

## Founder covenants

Founder to notify within three business days of single new debt/guarantee ≥RMB3m or 12-month aggregate ≥RMB8m, and of material attachment, freeze, enforcement, major disposals, material default or other material impairment irrespective of threshold. Related or split transactions aggregated. No unreasonable dissipation of material assets designed to impair repurchase performance.

## Repurchase

If no Qualified IPO by 31 Dec 2030, investor may require founder (not issuer under this proposed structure) to purchase some or all investor equity. Price for each funded tranche = corresponding paid principal × (1.08)^(actual days from its payment to repurchase payment /365) minus cash dividends attributable to the repurchased equity; avoid double counting. Pay within 30 days of valid notice, subject to definitive documentation and applicable law. This is not guaranteed cash recovery.

## Credit enhancement

Founder sister personal guarantee subject to documented personal asset/liability DD and investor acceptance. Property mortgage is not assumed: Bank B has not consented to proposed further mortgage. If agreed enhancement fails, second closing remains blocked absent satisfactory substitute or written waiver. Guarantee scope, form, term and enforcement to be separately documented; avoid suggesting guarantee effectiveness automatically depends on ongoing asset disclosure.

## IPO and exit

Pre-filing review of all investor special rights against then-applicable regulatory requirements; execute genuine termination/amendment if required; no automatic revival of rights terminated for IPO compliance merely upon unsuccessful IPO. Transfer restrictions, pre-emption and tag rights remain subject to negotiation and applicable law.

## Binding status

Indicative commercial provisions only unless expressly designated binding in the definitive documents. Confidentiality, exclusivity (if agreed), expenses, governing law and dispute resolution may be separately designated binding; this sample does not assume an exclusivity agreement exists. All terms subject to definitive agreements and legal review.
