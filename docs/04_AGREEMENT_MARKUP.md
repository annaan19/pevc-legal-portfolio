## 说明 / Legend

以下为条款级模拟修改，并非完整投资协议。删除内容用“原拟稿”，建议内容用“修订稿”区分；实际交易应以正式Redline文件复核交叉引用。

**English**

Clause-level educational mark-up, not a complete executed agreement. “Original” and “Revised” distinguish proposed wording; cross-references must be reconciled in definitive documentation.

## 第4条｜付款 / Clause 4 — Funding

【原拟稿】投资人签约时一次性支付3,000万元。
【修订稿】投资款分两期支付：第一期2,000万元，第二期1,000万元；各期增资及股权登记以实际付款为基础。

**English**

[ORIGINAL] Investor shall pay RMB30 million upon signing.
[REVISED] Subscription shall be funded in two tranches: RMB20 million at First Closing and RMB10 million at Second Closing. Corporate and equity records shall reflect amounts actually funded.

## 第4.3条｜先决条件 / Clause 4.3 — CP

【新增】第二期付款以增信安排依法签署并达到双方约定的客观证据要求、更新尽调及内部批准完成为前提；投资人可书面豁免其利益条件。未满足条件不当然导致整个投资协议自动终止。

**English**

[ADD] Second Closing is conditional upon duly documented credit support meeting objective evidentiary criteria, update DD and final approvals. Investor may waive investor-benefit CP in writing. Non-satisfaction does not automatically terminate the entire agreement.

## 第8条｜回购主体 / Clause 8 — Obligor

【原拟稿】目标公司及创始人共同回购。
【修订稿】由创始人依约受让投资人股权；目标公司不承担本拟议回购义务。

**English**

[ORIGINAL] The Company and Founder shall jointly repurchase.
[REVISED] The Founder shall purchase the Investor Equity pursuant to Clause 8; the Company is not a purchase obligor.

## 第8.2条｜价格 / Clause 8.2 — Price

【新增】每期投资款分别以其实际支付日为起点按8%年复利计算，扣除对应股权现金分红；部分回购按对应本金比例计算。

**English**

[ADD] Calculate each tranche separately at an 8% annual compound rate from its actual funding date, less cash dividends attributable to the purchased equity; allocate principal pro rata for partial purchases.

## 第8.3条｜通知 / Clause 8.3 — Notices

【新增】创始人知悉单笔新增借款或担保达到300万元、12个月累计达到800万元，或发生重大查封、执行、重大资产处置等事项后三个工作日内书面通知。

**English**

[ADD] Founder shall notify within three Business Days of becoming aware of any single new borrowing/guarantee of RMB3 million or more, aggregate RMB8 million or more within 12 months, or any material attachment, enforcement or disposal.

## 第9条｜保证 / Clause 9 — Guarantee

【新增】第三方保证的范围、方式、期间及争议解决由独立保证文件明确。保证人应为其本人义务签署；不能仅凭主协议要求其承担未同意的披露义务。

**English**

[ADD] Scope, form, period and enforcement of third-party guarantee must be in a separately executed guarantee instrument. No direct duty is imposed on a non-signatory guarantor merely by this agreement.

## IPO条款 / IPO provision

【修订】申报前按届时监管要求核查并处理特殊权利。已为IPO合规真实终止的权利，不约定因上市失败自动恢复。

**English**

[REVISED] Review and address special rights before filing under then-applicable rules. Rights genuinely terminated for IPO compliance do not automatically revive if listing is unsuccessful.
