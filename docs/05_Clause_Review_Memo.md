# Clause Review Memorandum

> Fictional investor-side legal work sample; not legal advice.

## To / From / Scope

To: Investment Committee, 远山成长基金 (fictional). From: Investor Legal Workstream (simulated). Subject: Project Aurora A-round investment: key clause issues and closing recommendation. This is a learning sample, not client advice.

## Question presented

Whether a RMB30m proposed A-round investment can proceed where original documents place repurchase obligations on both issuer and founder, the founder has encumbered assets and limited demonstrated payment capacity, and the issuer expects an A-share IPO.

## Short answer

PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS, not unconditional funding. Retain founder-only repurchase as ancillary protection, split funding into RMB20m + RMB10m, require verified enhancement, clarify continuing disclosure and staged remedies, and review special rights before IPO filing.

## Validity versus performance

Analyze separately: (1) formation and validity of the repurchase agreement; (2) whether the selected obligor may legally perform under company capital maintenance, distributions, creditor protection and relevant judicial rules; (3) whether a judgment or award could be practically enforced against available assets; and (4) whether the special right creates IPO review concerns. A contract can be valid but difficult to perform or collect. No blanket conclusion that all founder VAMs are prohibited or that issuer VAMs are always void.

## Credit risk

The illustrative five-year amount is RMB30m×1.08^5 ≈ RMB44.08m if all funds were paid five years before payment. Because this transaction funds RMB20m and RMB10m at different dates, actual contractual repurchase must be calculated separately for each tranche. Founder’s already pledged target equity and mortgaged property cannot be valued at nominal amounts. Sister’s RMB22.5m preliminary identifiable personal net assets do not establish full coverage of RMB44m; Qiming book net assets are not cash or sister’s personal assets.

## Closing analysis

Commercial dispute of RMB8m with estimated loss RMB3–5m, 8% revenue decline and disclosed founder new debts do not alone establish MAC. The critical unsatisfied CP is an effective investor-acceptable enhancement: sister’s asset DD remains incomplete and planned further property mortgage is unavailable. Investment committee approval is expressly conditional. Do not pay the second RMB10m until CP is met or validly waived and internal approval confirmed.

## IPO special rights

An automatic suspension/revival provision does not necessarily achieve actual clearing where required. Conduct pre-filing review under then-applicable rules; execute genuine termination/amendment if required, and avoid automatic revival of rights terminated for IPO compliance.

## Recommendations / open issues

Obtain original corporate files, shareholding/contribution evidence and pledge records; complete guarantor DD, assess alternatives and document guarantee; reconcile tranche equity mechanics; complete updated litigation and debt checks; finalize defined terms, warranties and CP; obtain documented investment committee approval; keep open issues explicit rather than implying legal compliance is already established.

## Research verification checklist

For any live use, recheck current PRC Company Law (including contribution and corporate guarantee rules), Civil Code contract and security provisions, current CSRC IPO guidance, exchange requirements and relevant judicial interpretations. The historic recruitment questions used as competency signals are not current legal authorities.
